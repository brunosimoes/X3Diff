X3D COVERAGE

What X3Diff compares

X3Diff compares %X3D_NODE_COUNT% X3D node types, listed below. It reads XML-encoded X3D 3.0, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3 and 4.0 with a catalog generated from the official X3D 3.3 model and schema. Anything outside the catalog is reported as not compared; it is never passed over in silence.

The %X3D_NODE_COUNT% node types

For each of these, the typed fields, their defaults, DEF/USE, ROUTE declarations and metadata are compared. Being in the list does not mean external content is fetched or runtime behaviour is evaluated.

What the status means

Complete
Supported declarations were compared without coverage errors or notes. This result applies to the declared comparison model and tolerances. The workbench shows no badge.
With notes
Everything inside the catalog was compared, with a caveat: for example a file that is not X3D 3.3, or external content that is declared but not loaded.
Incomplete
Some content was not compared: a node type outside the list above, an unsupported setting, an invalid value, or objects that could not be matched with confidence. The reported changes cover supported declarations, with uncertain matches identified; skipped content may contain further differences.
Failed
A file could not be read (not well-formed XML, or a version outside 3.0 to 4.0), so nothing was compared.

Outside the catalog

A node type that is not listed is skipped as a whole, together with everything inside it. This includes prototypes (PROTO/EXTERNPROTO), scripts, fog and unit declarations in the document header. Component requirements are compared as document details. External scenes and media are declared but never fetched. “No differences found” is meaningful only for the content that was compared, at the tolerances in use.

The geometry views (Slice & measure, Surface deviation, Volume differences) measure Box, Sphere, closed Cylinder and bounded convex IndexedFaceSet shapes under Group and Transform placement. Motion paths follows TimeSensor, PositionInterpolator and OrientationInterpolator animation routed to a Transform.
